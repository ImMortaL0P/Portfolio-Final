    05. Process

     

    Four steps, one person, a date agreed at the start and held to. A brand identity runs three to four weeks; a full site, six to ten.

    01 Brief Fixed scope We write down what is in and what is out before anything starts. The number I quote is the number you pay.
    02 Design Designed, then built Directions, then one chosen route taken all the way. You see real layouts with your own content, not filler.
    03 Build One point of contact The person who drew it is the person who builds it. No handoff, no translation loss, no third party to chase.
    04 Handover Yours to run Source files, fonts and an admin panel where the content actually changes — so routine updates never come back to me.
