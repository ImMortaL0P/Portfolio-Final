Redraw Lab

Seven cartoon characters rebuilt from scratch in vector — no tracing, no gradients, one line weight per figure. Drawing someone else's character is the fastest way to find out whether you can actually hold a silhouette. Read left to right, top to bottom.

Colophon

Each figure was rebuilt on a fresh artboard in Illustrator, working from memory and reference rather than tracing — the exercise is silhouette, line weight and how few anchor points a shape can survive on. Everything here is a study.

The characters belong to their respective creators and studios; these redraws are practice pieces, not original characters and not for sale.
